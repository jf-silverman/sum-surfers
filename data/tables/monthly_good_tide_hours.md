# Daylight hours with a surfable tide, by month

A "good tide" here is under 3.5 ft, the rough threshold this spot surfs best below.
Hours are counted between real dawn and dusk for each date, over the hours the
pipeline actually observed, so a short collection day counts fewer hours than it had.
Months with no footage are interpolated from the nearest month either side.
